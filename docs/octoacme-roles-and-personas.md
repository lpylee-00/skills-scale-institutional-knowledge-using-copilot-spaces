# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance / Testing Leads own the quality strategy, test planning, and validation of features against acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans, test cases, and quality gates
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify, document, and triage defects
- Validate that acceptance criteria are met before release sign-off
- Define test-environment and test-data requirements

### Goals
- Ensure quality standards are met before release
- Reduce post-release defects and customer issues
- Enable fast feedback loops during development

### Typical Communication
- Plan test coverage with Developers and Product Managers during refinement and sprint planning
- Share test progress, defects, and blockers with Developers and Project Managers in team ceremonies
- Confirm release readiness and quality risks with Product Managers and Project Managers

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches own facilitation of agile practices and continuous improvement, helping the team collaborate effectively and remove delivery impediments.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Coach the team on agreed agile practices and ways of working
- Identify and help remove process impediments
- Promote continuous improvement through retrospective actions
- Protect the team from unplanned disruption while making impediments visible

### Goals
- Maintain predictable, sustainable delivery
- Improve team collaboration and flow of work
- Ensure process impediments have clear owners and follow-through

### Typical Communication
- Facilitate delivery ceremonies for Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers
- Partner with Project Managers to surface dependencies and unblock delivery work
- Coach Product Managers and Developers on backlog readiness, estimation, and feedback loops

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects own system solution design and technical standards, ensuring delivered systems are scalable, secure, maintainable, and aligned with technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and component interactions
- Define technical standards, patterns, and best practices
- Review technical designs for architectural impact
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide technology selections and integration strategies

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and architectural rework
- Enable future feature velocity and flexibility

### Typical Communication
- Collaborate with Developers on design reviews, implementation trade-offs, and technical standards
- Advise Product Managers on feasibility, technical constraints, and sequencing decisions
- Provide Project Managers with technical dependencies, estimates, and risk information

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security / Compliance Officers own security, compliance, and risk-management requirements for the project and verify that delivery meets applicable obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements and control expectations
- Assess security, privacy, and regulatory risks
- Review designs and releases for security and compliance readiness
- Coordinate risk treatment, exceptions, and evidence collection
- Escalate unresolved material risks to the appropriate decision owner

### Goals
- Protect customer, company, and project information
- Ensure compliance obligations are met before release
- Reduce security and compliance risk throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Define security and compliance acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Review technical risks and mitigations with Developers and Technical Architects
- Report compliance status, exceptions, and unresolved risks to Project Managers and stakeholders

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts own the clarification and traceability of business requirements, bridging stakeholder needs with technical implementation details.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, analyze, and document business requirements
- Translate business needs into clear workflows, rules, and acceptance criteria
- Maintain traceability between requirements, decisions, and delivered outcomes
- Identify requirement gaps, assumptions, and dependencies
- Support validation that delivered capabilities meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are complete, clear, and testable
- Reduce rework caused by ambiguity or missed business rules
- Align delivery decisions with intended business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Refine requirements and acceptance criteria with Product Managers and Developers
- Coordinate requirement dependencies and clarification decisions with Project Managers
- Validate workflows and assumptions with stakeholders before and during implementation

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders / Executive Sponsors own strategic direction, resource sponsorship, and timely decisions needed to keep the project aligned with business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Set strategic outcomes and approve major scope or investment decisions
- Provide resources and organizational support
- Resolve escalated cross-team priorities and risks
- Review progress against business outcomes and success metrics
- Champion adoption and communicate strategic context

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Remove organizational barriers to successful delivery

### Typical Communication
- Review roadmap, outcomes, and priority trade-offs with Product Managers
- Receive status, risks, dependencies, and escalation requests from Project Managers
- Provide decisions and strategic context that guide Developers and the wider delivery team
